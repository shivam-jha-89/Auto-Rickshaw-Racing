    About Rikshaw Rush

    A 3D endless runner through Karachi — Burns Road, Sea View, Kemari Harbour, Shahrah-e-Faisal and Mazar-e-Quaid — built entirely in the browser.

    Engineering highlights
    • Zero asset files. Every model, texture and sound is generated in code at startup: geometry is baked per material into 2–4 draw calls per object, textures are painted on <canvas>, and all audio is synthesised with the Web Audio API.
    • Curved-world shader. A vertex-shader bend makes the straight track drop over the horizon and sway, while gameplay stays in straight space.
    • Fair procedural spawner. Traffic speed is a fraction of the player's, so every obstacle has a fixed arrival distance — the generator can guarantee an open lane and that vehicles never clip through each other.
    • Dynamic world. Day/night cycle, rain and dust storms, lightning, bloom post-processing, animated water, drifting clouds and a distant skyline.
    • Systems. Combos and fever mode, AI rival racer, passenger missions, power-ups, daily challenges, achievements, garage economy, lucky spin, radio with your own playlist (IndexedDB).
    • Platform. Clerk sign-in with cloud save, installable PWA with offline play, responsive on phone, tablet and desktop, touch + keyboard controls, optional Blender .glb model overrides.

    How to Play

    • ←→ / AD / swipe — lane badlo
    • ↑ W Space / swipe up — jump (potholes & barriers)
    • In the air press jump again — 360° trick (+score, +nitro)
    • ↓ S / swipe down — brake, or slam down in the air
    • Shift N / double-tap / ⚡ — Nitro: smash through everything
    • H / tap — horn: the car ahead moves aside (trucks & cows don't care!)
    • Near misses & tricks build a combo multiplier up to x6
    • Hail-waving sawari: drive in the edge lane next to them, then reach the drop-off before time runs out
    • Wooden ramps launch you over traffic. Puddles slow you down.
    • Power-ups: 🧲 Magnet · 🛡 Shield · 2x Coins · ⚡ Nitro canister
    • 🔤 K-A-R-A-C-H-I letters jama karo — roz ka jackpot Rs 2500!
    • 🛺 Chotu Ustad race lagaye to use overtake karo
    • 🔥 x6 combo pe FEVER: double score + double coins
    • B Headstart · V Shield Start (run ke pehle 7 second)
    • R radio station badlo · P/Esc pause · M mute
